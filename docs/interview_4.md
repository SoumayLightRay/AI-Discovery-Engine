# Interview 4 — Transcript Analysis

> **Respondent:** Aastha Srivastav, Female
> **Platform:** Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart (cross-platform comparer)
> **Wishlist usage:** "Watch later" — genuine purchase intent, not aspirational
> **Interview date:** Sep 5, 2026

---

## Key Quotes

**On why items sit in wishlist:**
> "I have a lot of the stuff that I was... the kind of stuff that I wanted to buy, I have a lot of those things. So I don't want to impulsively buy it."
> "It's kind of like watch later होता है, it's kind of like that."

**On quality/reviews:**
> "For the quality, I like to look at the reviews of the product, so that I can find out if it's reliable or not."

**On paying more for confidence:**
> Bought a top from **Amazon at a higher price** instead of Flipkart because Amazon had better reviews — **same product**.
> "On Flipkart it was cheaper and it did not have much reviews. But on Amazon it was a bit expensive, but it did have good reviews. So I got it from Amazon then."

**On cross-platform comparison:**
> "For price comparison and quality comparison by reviews."

**On return policy:**
> "I always check the return policy. अगर नहीं होता return का, तो I don't order from them क्योंकि I find it very suspicious."

**On buyer photos vs. model pics:**
> "I don't trust the model pics at all क्योंकि it has happened with me बहुत times कि model pics से बिल्कुल ही अलग आता है वो, the product that I'm ordering. So I rely on the reviews, में जो buyers अपने pictures डालते हैं, उस पे."

**On what would make her decide faster:**
> Stock/availability reminders: "There should be reminders कि अगर if this product is going to go out of stock, या फिर इसमें some particular sizes have gone out of stock... and when it would be restocked."

---

## Key Findings

1. **Confirms confidence > price** — bought same top from Amazon at higher price because better reviews (3rd user to confirm this pattern)
2. **Cross-platform comparison for both price AND reviews** — not just price
3. **Strongly distrusts model photos** — has received products that look nothing like model pics, relies entirely on buyer photos
4. **Return policy = trust signal** — no return policy = "suspicious", won't order
5. **Wishlist = genuine intent** — explicitly says she only wishlists items she plans to buy later
6. **Wants availability/stock alerts** — reminders for out-of-stock and restock notifications (aligns with availability segment, 11%)
