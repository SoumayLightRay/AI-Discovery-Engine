# Interview 9 — Transcript Analysis

> **Respondent:** Tanej, Male
> **Platform:** Amazon (primary for clothes), Myntra
> **Wishlist usage:** Budget-waiting — wishlists when can't afford, buys later
> **Interview date:** Sep 5, 2026

---

## Key Quotes

**On why items sit in wishlist:**
> "I was not able to afford that at that time."
> Secondary: "I was thinking that how it will look on me." — fit uncertainty (XL vs XXL confusion)

**On paying more for confidence:**
> Bought a sweatshirt from **Zara online** instead of a cheaper identical-looking option from another brand.
> "I purchased from Zara because of Zara, right?" — brand trust > price.

**On cross-platform comparison:**
> "I have done this many times." — checks other apps for discount options.

**On return policy:**
> "Return policy तो mostly same ही होती है सबकी."

**On feature ideas:**
> 1. **3D modeling / virtual try-on** — "Open the camera, put it in front of you, you can see how it will look on you. That will give them clarity."
> 2. **Myntra Quality Check badge** — for products with no reviews: "जो अच्छे quality check को pass कर रहे हैं, उसको एक अलग label लगा दे कि these are standardized or checked quality check clothes."

**On zero reviews:**
> "That's maybe a problem because reviews must be there. बिना reviews को confirm नहीं होता."

**On Cross-Seller Review Bridge (when pitched):**
> "Maybe yes. That will give me the real, real picture of the product."

**On seller reviews alone:**
> "Seller का review इतना matter नहीं करेगा, because if the person is selling, he wants to sell his item." — doesn't trust seller self-reporting.

---

## Key Findings

1. **Budget-waiting is primary delay** — but fit uncertainty is secondary blocker (XL vs XXL)
2. **Confirms confidence > price** — bought Zara sweatshirt at premium over identical cheaper option. Brand = trust = confidence
3. **Myntra Quality Check badge — NOVEL IDEA** — for zero-review products, Myntra could certify quality with a verification badge. User acknowledged price may increase but would accept that tradeoff
4. **Cross-Seller Review Bridge — tepid acceptance** — "Maybe yes" but with reservations ("can be blurry and something")
5. **Doesn't trust seller self-reviews** — "he wants to sell his item." Wants independent verification (quality check mark)
6. **Virtual try-on requested (2nd user)** — same as Rishika (Int 1), suggests this is a recurring user need
