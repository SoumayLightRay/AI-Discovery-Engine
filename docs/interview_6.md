# Interview 6 — Transcript Analysis

> **Respondent:** Steve, Male (interviewer's best friend)
> **Platform:** Myntra (very infrequent — last used ~3 months ago)
> **Wishlist usage:** Rare — prefers offline shopping, uses wishlist minimally
> **Interview date:** Sep 5, 2026

---

## Key Quotes

**On why items sit in wishlist:**
> "Because I wanted to see the offline market so that I can get a better bargain, like a better product and a better price." (Cargo pants)

**On preferring offline:**
> "I prefer to see things myself, and if I can find the clothes offline and try them myself before buying it, rather than buying it online before having to try it on."

**On paying more for confidence:**
> "Yes... I would rather check for the comments to see if the product is valid and of good quality, even if it's slightly more expensive."
> Phone cover example: "Usually you see that there are so many phone covers on Amazon, but when you eventually receive them, they never look exactly the way shown in the picture. So I prefer to look at the comments, pictures from people who have already bought it."

**On return policy:**
> "I do check for return policy to see if it's an option in case it doesn't fit or it doesn't look good on me."

**On what would make him decide:**
> "Good discount." (monetary — primary)
> Non-monetary: "To be honest, I am not a very compulsive buyer, so even if something is extremely beautiful aesthetically, I would still not buy it immediately. I would take a few days to think about it and verify the price."

---

## Key Findings

1. **Offline-first shopper** — wishlists online then checks offline market for the same/similar product. Different leakage vector: not to competitor apps, but to **physical retail**
2. **Confirms confidence > price (5th user)** — pays more for reviewed items (phone cover example), checks buyer photos because "they never look exactly the way shown in the picture"
3. **Very deliberate buyer** — takes days to decide even for aesthetically appealing items. Not impulsive
4. **Return policy matters** — checks it before buying, standard practice
5. **Not a cross-platform digital comparer** — doesn't check the exact same item across apps. Looks for "other options" broadly
6. **Price is primary trigger** — but this user is honestly outside our target segment (very infrequent online fashion buyer)

---

## Confirms: Confidence > price (5th confirmation — buyer photos over model pics, pays more for reviewed items)
