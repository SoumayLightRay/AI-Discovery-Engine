# Interview 8 — Transcript Analysis

> **Respondent:** Himanshi, Female
> **Platform:** Urbanic (primary), Amazon, Flipkart
> **Wishlist usage:** Comparison shortlisting — saves items while looking for better designs
> **Interview date:** Sep 5, 2026

---

## Key Quotes

**On why items sit in wishlist:**
> "Just want to see the comparison between the jeans... different different jeans."
> "उससे भी अच्छा मिल सकता है product. इसलिए उसके बारे में research करना... कि और भी कुछ अलग design हो, कुछ ज़्यादा attractive मिल सके."

**On paying more for confidence:**
> "हाँ, expensive choose किया है" — chooses more expensive items on Urbanic (₹1000–1500 range) because quality is good. Bought a dress ~3 months ago.

**On cross-platform comparison:**
> "हाँ, मैं comparison भी करती हूँ." — Compares for reviews, return policy, and to find where product is better available.

**On what would help on wishlist page:**
> "अगर comparison वहीं हो, reviews भी वहीं हो product के मतलब सब कुछ वहीं आ जाए... अलग-अलग जगह हमें research नहीं करनी पड़ेगी." (All info on one page so no need to research elsewhere.)

**On buyer photos vs. model pics:**
> Trusts model pics on Urbanic because quality is consistently good — doesn't doubt them. But still checks reviews for other platforms.

**On zero reviews:**
> "नहीं, क्योंकि ये तो देखना ही पड़ता है."

**On Cross-Seller Review Bridge (when pitched):**
> "हम still नहीं खरीदेंगे. वो doubt तो बना रहता ही है. मैं फिर भी नहीं खरीदूँगी."
> "हमारे पास और भी options होते हैं, तो हम क्यों ही... 1% भी doubt नहीं रखना चाहेंगे ना मन में?"

---

## Key Findings

1. **Wishlist = design comparison tool** — saves items to find something even more attractive. "ये भी अच्छा था, option मैं रखा"
2. **Confirms confidence > price** — chooses expensive Urbanic items for quality
3. **Cross-Seller Review Bridge REJECTED** — will NOT buy a zero-review product even if seller's other products have great reviews. "1% भी doubt नहीं रखना चाहेंगे." First clear rejection of this concept
4. **Wants all info consolidated on one page** — explicitly asked for comparison + reviews + everything on the wishlist page so she doesn't have to research separately
5. **Platform trust can substitute for review trust** — trusts Urbanic model pics because the platform has consistently delivered quality. Doesn't need buyer photos on a trusted platform
6. **Zero reviews = no buy** — absolute, no exceptions, even with seller reputation

---

## Segment: Design-comparison shopper with zero-tolerance for unreviewed products
## Confirms: Confidence > price (chose expensive Urbanic for quality)
## Confirms: Cross-platform comparison (reviews + return policy + better options)
## REJECTS: Cross-Seller Review Bridge — will not buy zero-review product regardless of seller reputation
## New insight: Platform-level trust (Urbanic) can replace product-level trust — but only for platforms with consistent quality track record
## New insight: Wants consolidated decision information on wishlist page — "research नहीं करनी पड़ेगी"
