# Weekly Discovery Pulse: Myntra Wishlist Conversion

**Total Dataset Size:** 1367 reviews & posts across Google Play, Apple App Store, YouTube, and Reddit.  
**Stratified Sample Analyzed:** 313 reviews.  
**Friction Rate:** 22.36% of analyzed touchpoints report active purchase blockers.

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The primary friction causing wishlist drop‑offs is a deep‑seated lack of confidence in the purchase process, manifested through missing transactional clarity, price uncertainty, and return anxiety, which pushes users to defer or abandon purchases.

**Dominant Friction Theme:** `Other emergent themes`

## 3. Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys)
- **Why 1**: Users hesitate to move items from wishlist to cart because they feel uncertain about the final purchase outcome.
- **Why 2**: That uncertainty originates from insufficient transparent information about pricing stability, invoicing, and return options.
- **Why 3**: When critical details are opaque, users experience anxiety about financial risk and post‑purchase regret.
- **Why 4**: Anxiety triggers procrastination; users keep items in the wishlist as a temporary safety net.
- **Why 5**: The safety‑net habit leads to prolonged indecision, during which alternative products or platforms become more attractive, resulting in drop‑off.

## 4. Opportunity Areas for Wishlist Conversion
### 💡 Opportunity: Other emergent themes
**Problem Statement:**
> Among first‑time shoppers who add items to their wishlist but never purchase, users who lack a clear, verifiable transaction record delay purchasing because they doubt the legitimacy and completeness of the order. They currently keep items in the wishlist as a placeholder, which leaves them uncertain and eventually leads to abandonment.

**Grounded Evidence (Verbatim Quote):**
*"Very disappointing experience with Myntra... no proper bill or invoice inside"*

### 💡 Opportunity: Price
**Problem Statement:**
> Among price‑sensitive users who bookmark items, users who anticipate future discounts delay purchasing because they expect price reductions that never materialize. They currently monitor prices manually or wait indefinitely, which leaves them frustrated and prone to discard the wishlist items.

**Grounded Evidence (Verbatim Quote):**
*"none of the good items got any discounts whatsoever"*

### 💡 Opportunity: Returns
**Problem Statement:**
> Among users who have previously faced return obstacles, users who add products to their wishlist delay purchasing because they fear they will be unable to return or exchange mismatched items. They currently avoid adding to cart and keep items in the wishlist, which leaves them stuck in indecision and increases the chance of eventual drop‑off.

**Grounded Evidence (Verbatim Quote):**
*"Myntra doesn't have a option to return mismatched products they rejected the exch"*

## 5. Primary Research Interview Guide (Validation Questions)
Use these targeted questions during user interviews:
1. "Can you walk me through the last time you added an item to your wishlist but didn’t move it to the cart? What thoughts or concerns held you back?"
1. "How do you perceive the information provided about pricing changes, invoices, and return policies when you consider buying a wishlisted item?"
1. "What would make you feel confident enough to convert a wishlisted product into a purchase without relying on external monitoring or work‑arounds?"
