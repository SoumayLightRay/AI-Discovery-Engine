# Quick 5-Minute Call Script — Wishlist Validation

> **Goal:** Validate the core problem (Information Asymmetry/Confidence) in a fast phone call.
> **Time:** 3–5 minutes max.

---

## 1. The Setup (30s)
"Hey, I'm doing a quick 3-minute research call for a fashion app project. Can I ask you a few rapid-fire questions about how you shop online?"

## 2. The Core Blocker (1 min)
"Think about the last time you saved or wishlisted an item on Myntra or AJIO but didn't end up buying it. 
**Why didn't you buy it?**" 
*(Listen, don't prompt. Note if they say price, fit, or quality/trust).*

## 3. The "Confidence vs. Price" Test (1 min)
"Have you ever bought something that was slightly MORE expensive than another option, just because it had better reviews or buyer photos?"
*(If Yes, ask for a quick example. This validates our core 'Confidence > Price' hypothesis).*

## 4. The Competitor Leakage Test (1 min)
"Have you ever liked an item on Myntra, but then left the app to check Amazon or AJIO for the exact same item? What were you looking for?"
*(Listen for: 'checking reviews', 'checking return policy', or 'checking price').*

## 5. The Magic Wand (30s)
"If I could add one piece of information to the wishlist page that would make you instantly decide whether to buy or drop an item, what would it be?"

---

## What you need them to say (Success Criteria):
- ✅ "I need to see real photos / I don't trust the model pics."
- ✅ "Yes, I paid more once because the cheaper one looked sketchy / had no reviews."
- ✅ "I check Amazon to see if the reviews are better."

If they say these things, the **Wishlist Confidence Layer MVP** is fully validated for them.
