# Primary Research Interview Script
## AI Discovery Engine — Myntra Wishlist Conversion

**Purpose:** Validate the root causes identified by the AI Discovery Engine through 5–6 targeted 1:1 user interviews.

**Target Segment:** Active Myntra users (18–35 years) who have used the wishlist feature in the last 30 days but have at least 3 items still pending in their wishlist.

**Methodology:** Semi-structured interview, 20–25 minutes. Open-ended questions only. No leading or suggestive framing.

**Critical Rule:** The interviewer must NOT reveal the hypothesis. Let the user surface the friction organically.

---

## Part 1: Warm-Up & Context (3 min)

1. "Tell me about the last time you used Myntra. What were you looking for?"
2. "How often do you add items to your wishlist? Walk me through why you do that instead of adding to cart."
3. "How many items are currently sitting in your wishlist right now?"

---

## Part 2: Purchase Decision Probing (8 min)

4. "Think of the last item you wishlisted but didn't buy. What happened? Walk me through the thought process."
5. "When you add an item to your wishlist, what thoughts about its price influence whether you move forward with purchase?"
   - *Follow-up:* "What would change your mind?"
6. "Have you ever removed something from your wishlist? Why?"
7. "What information do you look at on the product page before deciding to buy vs. skip?"

---

## Part 3: Quality & Material Confidence (5 min)
*Theme validated by Discovery Engine: Quality/Material = 7.7% friction density*

8. "How do you assess the quality of a product when its price seems high? What information would make you feel more confident?"
   - *Follow-up:* "Have you ever received something that didn't match what you expected from the listing?"
9. "What would need to change about the product page for you to feel confident enough to buy immediately instead of wishlisting?"

---

## Part 4: Returns & Risk Perception (5 min)
*Theme validated by Discovery Engine: Returns = 3.6% friction density*

10. "What concerns, if any, do you have about returning items, and how does that affect your decision to buy?"
    - *Follow-up for intimate wear:* "Have you ever hesitated to buy bras, underwear, or similar items online? What held you back?"
11. "If you could design the ideal return policy for fashion items, what would it look like?"

---

## Part 5: Cool-Down & Open Discovery (4 min)

12. "If Myntra could change one thing to make you buy from your wishlist faster, what would it be?"
13. "Is there anything else about your shopping experience that frustrates you that we haven't talked about?"

---

## Analysis Framework

After conducting all 5–6 interviews, map each response to:

| Response | Root Cause Category | Frequency (n/6) | Validates Engine? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "I wasn't sure about the fabric" | Quality/Material | ? | ✅/❌ |
| "No returns on innerwear" | Returns Friction | ? | ✅/❌ |
| "Waiting for a sale" | Price | ? | ✅/❌ |
| [New finding] | [New theme] | ? | 🆕 |

**Success Criteria:** If ≥ 4/6 users independently surface Quality/Material or Returns as a top-2 friction, the Discovery Engine hypothesis is validated for PRD development.
