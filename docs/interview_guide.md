# User Interview Script — Wishlist Behaviour
> **Time needed:** 10–15 minutes per person
> **Who to interview:** Anyone who shops fashion online (Myntra, AJIO, Amazon, Flipkart)
> **Tip:** Record audio (with permission) so you can transcript later. Keep it conversational.

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

"Hey, I'm working on a project about how people shop for fashion online. Can I ask you a few quick questions? It'll take 10 minutes max. No right or wrong answers — I just want to understand how YOU shop."

---

## BLOCK 1: Wishlist Behaviour (3 mins)

**Q1.** "Do you use the wishlist / heart button on any fashion app?"
→ If no: "How do you save items you like?" → End early or pivot to bookmarking behaviour.

**Q2.** "Can you open your wishlist right now and show me? How many items are in there?"

**Q3.** "Pick the most recent item you saved but HAVEN'T bought yet. What is it?"

**Q4.** "Why haven't you bought it yet? In your own words."
→ **DO NOT suggest reasons.** Let them talk. Probe with: "Tell me more about that."

---

## BLOCK 2: The Confidence Question (3 mins)

**Q5.** "When you look at that item right now, what would you need to know or see to feel confident enough to buy it TODAY?"
→ Probe: "Is it about the price? The quality? How it looks on you? Something else?"

**Q6.** "Have you ever bought something that was MORE expensive than a similar option because the reviews or photos made you trust it more?"
→ If yes: "Tell me about that — what did you see that convinced you?"
→ (This is the "Blue Cobalt Shorts" question — testing if confidence > price)

**Q7.** "Have you ever decided NOT to buy something specifically because there were no reviews or no customer photos?"

---

## BLOCK 3: The Research Journey (2 mins)

**Q8.** "Before buying a fashion item, do you check anything OUTSIDE the app? Like YouTube, Instagram, Amazon, Reddit, or asking friends?"
→ Probe: "What exactly are you looking for that isn't on the product page?"

**Q9.** "Have you ever found the same item on another app (like Amazon or AJIO) and bought it there instead? Why?"
→ (Testing competitor leakage)

---

## BLOCK 4: What Would Help (2 mins)

**Q10.** "If you could add ONE feature to your wishlist that would help you decide faster, what would it be?"
→ **DO NOT suggest ideas.** Let them think.
→ If they say "price drop alert": "Okay, but imagine price can't change. What else?"

**Q11.** "Imagine Myntra showed you: real photos from people who bought this item, a percentage saying '85% say true to size', and a clear badge saying 'returnable' or 'non-returnable'. Would that change how quickly you buy?"

---

## Closing (30 seconds)

"That was super helpful, thank you! One last thing — would you be open to a quick follow-up if I have one more question later?"

---

## What to Listen For (Your Mental Checklist)

While they talk, mentally note which of these patterns they match:

| Pattern | Listen for... |
|---|---|
| **Value-unproven** | "Not sure it's worth it", "looks different in real life" |
| **Fit/style doubtful** | "Don't know if it'll suit me", "size charts are wrong" |
| **Cross-platform comparer** | "I check Amazon too", "I found it cheaper on..." |
| **Price-waiter** | "Waiting for sale", "too expensive right now" |
| **Stock-blocked** | "My size isn't available" |
| **Passive decayer** | "I forgot about it", "just haven't gotten around to it" |
| **Confidence > Price** | Bought the pricier option because of reviews ← GOLD |

---

## After Each Interview

Immediately write down (even voice note to yourself):
1. Which segment do they fit?
2. What was their ONE strongest blocker?
3. Did they confirm or contradict our hypothesis?
4. Any surprising quote worth using in the deck?
