# Interview 10 — Transcript Analysis

> **Respondent:** Sia Maeshwari, Female
> **Platform:** Multiple apps — compares across many before buying
> **Wishlist usage:** Consideration holding — wishlists when confused about product authenticity
> **Interview date:** Sep 5, 2026

---

## Key Quotes

**On why items sit in wishlist:**
> "I was confused whether should I buy this product or not... whether the item will be the same exact that is shown in the picture."

**On paying more for confidence:**
> Bought a party dress (~₹2000) that was more expensive because it had better reviews. Checked offline AND online across multiple apps before deciding.

**On cross-platform comparison:**
> "I check everything, like price, return policy, everything. I check many apps before buying from one website."

**On buyer photos vs. model pics:**
> "I saw review photo that people put there. That is the thing I need to check."

**On Cross-Seller Review Bridge (when pitched):**
> "Yeah. I do that." — already checks seller's other products naturally.

**On zero reviews:**
> "I don't go to review that time. If it suits me, then I add it to wishlist." — will still wishlist a zero-review item but the uncertainty remains unresolved.

---

## Key Findings

1. **Quality/authenticity doubt is primary blocker** — "whether the item will be the same exact that is shown in the picture." Price was NOT the issue. Directly validates our root cause
2. **Confirms confidence > price** — bought more expensive dress because better reviews. Also checked offline stores before committing online
3. **Heavy cross-platform researcher** — checks price, return policy, reviews across many apps before buying from one. Aligns with survey finding (89% research externally)
4. **Buyer photos are essential** — "review photo that people put there, that is the thing I need to check"
5. **Cross-Seller Review Bridge — naturally does this already** — "Yeah, I do that." Validates the concept as matching existing user behaviour
6. **Offline + online research** — checked offline stores AND online reviews before buying the dress. Multi-channel decision process
