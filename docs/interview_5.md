# Interview 5 — Transcript Analysis

> **Respondent:** Umesh, Male
> **Platform:** Myntra, Amazon (uses both frequently)
> **Wishlist usage:** Sale/discount waiting — clubs multiple products for bank discounts
> **Interview date:** Sep 5, 2026

---

## Key Quotes

**On why items sit in wishlist:**
> "Usually I am waiting for the sale or any sort of discount, or I have to club multiple products such that I can get bank discount."

**On paying more for confidence:**
> "Yes, I'm willing to pay more."
> "There are many similar items from Vimal versus Mubaro versus Bewakoof, and I prefer to go with Bewakoof even if it's expensive for the joggers. And similar I go for Snitch many times. Even the item look same on Amazon from a cheaper seller — I prefer to buy the shirts from Snitch only."

**On buyer photos vs. model pics:**
> "I definitely need to see the real photos."

**On zero reviews:**
> "I will prefer to check the return policy and refund policy and branding. The brand is famous, like Snitch or Bewakoof or whatever. I will still trust it."

**On Cross-Seller Review Bridge (when pitched):**
> "Yeah, yeah. If reviews are not there, what matters is return policy."

**On what would make him decide faster:**
> "Sort items based upon the recent discount, how much it got lower based upon the price I added in the wishlist."
> (Non-monetary): "Recent orders increased. Recently a lot of people started buying. It builds the confidence, and very high return rate."

---

## Key Findings

1. **Confirms confidence > price (4th user)** — pays more for Bewakoof/Snitch over cheaper identical-looking items because of brand trust + reviews
2. **Brand trust substitutes for reviews** — if brand is known (Snitch, Bewakoof), will trust even without reviews. Aligns with Aziz (Int 3)
3. **Sale-waiting is primary delay** — explicitly clubs products for bank discounts. Pure price-timing behaviour
4. **Wants social proof signals** — unprompted, asked for "recent orders increased" and "high return rate" as confidence builders
5. **Return policy = fallback when no reviews** — same pattern as Aziz (Int 3) and Aastha (Int 4)
6. **Definitely needs real photos** — won't trust model pics alone
