# Interview 7 — Transcript Analysis

> **Respondent:** Nandini, Female
> **Platform:** Myntra, Amazon, Ajio, Nykaa (cross-platform comparer)
> **Wishlist usage:** Infrequent — once every 2–3 months. Uses it for comparison + waiting for money/reviews
> **Interview date:** Sep 5, 2026

---

## Key Quotes

**On why items sit in wishlist:**
> "To compare the better options and prices."
> "Wishlist is basically something you're confused about or you want to wait for the money and good reviews."

**On paying more for confidence:**
> "Yes, absolutely." — Bought a dress ~20–30% more expensive because it had good reviews and good pictures.

**On cross-platform comparison:**
> "There are times when I compare usually bags and footwear among all of these pages, because sometimes the images can be copied from somewhere or they are not real. So you never know."
> "Amazon or Myntra, somehow they use same pictures for two-three products. So you have to check if it is real or not."

**On zero reviews — will NOT buy:**
> "I don't think I'll buy it until unless I know the person whose brand it is."
> "Have you ever decided not to buy something specifically because it had no reviews or customer photos? Yes."

**On external research for zero-review products:**
> "I do the research, maybe I'll see on Instagram if I have to buy, other people are wearing or not wearing, or using the product."
> "If it's available elsewhere and there are reviews, because some or the other platform will have a review. If they don't have, I'll not end up buying it."

**On Cross-Seller Review Bridge (when pitched):**
> "Then I'll go ahead and buy it. If the rating is good, I'll buy it... I will buy if it has a return policy."

**On buyer photos vs. model pics:**
> "Real images. Real buyer images."

**On what would help on wishlist page:**
> "Comparisons. Or maybe similar products."

---

## Key Findings

1. **Confirms confidence > price (6th user)** — paid 20–30% more for a dress with good reviews + photos
2. **Zero reviews = absolute no-buy** — won't purchase unless she knows the brand founder personally. Strongest "no reviews no buy" signal across all interviews
3. **Cross-platform image verification** — checks if product images are real or copied across platforms. Trust issue with product photography itself
4. **External research cascade** — Instagram → other platforms → if no reviews anywhere, won't buy at all
5. **Cross-Seller Review Bridge validated (3rd user)** — will buy zero-review product if seller rating is good + return policy exists
6. **Wishlist = confusion + waiting** — her definition: "something you're confused about or you want to wait for money and good reviews"
7. **Wants comparison features** — asked for comparisons and similar products on wishlist page
